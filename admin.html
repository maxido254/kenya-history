Add a book

Fill this in, press the button, and you get two files to upload to your GitHub repo. This page cannot publish anything by itself.

Or paste the text below. Start each chapter with a line like Chapter 1 or # Chapter title. Sub-headings can start with ##.

The app only shows books that have both of these filled in.

Your files

Download the book file Download books.json

  1. On GitHub, open your kenya-history repo, then the books folder, choose Add file, then Upload files, and add the book file.
  2. Go back to the top of the repo and upload books.json the same way, replacing the old one.
  3. Commit. In a minute or two the book appears in the app for everyone, the next time they open it.

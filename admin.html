Add books

Add up to 6 books at a time. You get one zip to upload to your GitHub repo. This page cannot publish anything by itself. Only add books you have permission to share.

Start each chapter with a line like Chapter 1 or # Chapter title. Sub-headings can start with ##.

Your files

Download everything (zip)

Or download the files one by one:

  1. Unzip the download on your computer. You get a books folder and books.json.
  2. On GitHub, open your kenya-history repo, choose Add file, then Upload files, and drag in the books folder and books.json together. Commit.
  3. Wait a minute or two. The books then appear in the app and download to phones on their own.
  4. Wait for them to appear before making the next batch, so the list stays complete.

Remove books

Take books off the library. Phones that already saved them clear them the next time they open the app.

Your files

Download books.json

  1. On GitHub, upload this books.json to the top of your repo, replacing the old one. The books disappear from the app.
  2. Then delete each book's file so it is no longer public. Open each link below, scroll down and press Commit changes.

    Deleting a file removes it from the site. GitHub still keeps older versions in the repo's history, so to wipe a book completely you would need to recreate the repo.
